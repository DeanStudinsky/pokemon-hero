🎮 Controls

📖 Gameplay

  • 🏰 Settlements produce units and resources
  • ⚔️ Build armies to conquer territory
  • ✨ Capture wild Pokemon on the map
  • 🏥 Pokemon Centers heal defeated Pokemon
  • ⭐ Pokemon have special abilities in battle
